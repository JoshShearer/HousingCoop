## 2026-10-22 — Structure analysis and market model
- Deliverable: Claude Doc "Friendstead Structure and Market Analysis" (link in README).
- Preliminary recommendation: development LLC -> plat -> fee-simple lots + Chapter 209 POA for the common tract; Chapter 251 co-op and tenancy in common rejected; condo regime kept as fallback if the county will not plat.
- Base case modeled: 6 households, 30 acres east/southeast of Austin, 2,000 sq ft homes, 2,400 sq ft barndo, paved 2,000 ft road.
- Headline: ~$650K per household co-op self-build vs ~$770K solo lot + contractor; ~$120K (16%) saving tied to ~1,500 labor hours each. Metro median existing home $421K (Unlock MLS, Sept 2026).
- Open questions for Josh: household count and cash per household; east vs Hill Country; owner-occupied vs rental; comparison baseline; trade licenses in the group; target home size.
- Source gaps: Williamson 2-acre OSSF rule and Hays Table 10-1 not opened (PDF/403); road cost is unsourced; land prices are asking prices from one broker site.
