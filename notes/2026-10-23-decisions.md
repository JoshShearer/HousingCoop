## Barndominium v4: back to 60 x 40 with a shared wet core
- Question raised: would a smaller house slab with higher walls and two stories be cheaper? Answer: not in a metal building. Slab and roof are the cheap parts; the second floor structure, stairs, taller insulated walls and working at height eat the saving. Roughly a wash before the extra member hours.
