# Friendstead Open Questions

Assumptions the numbers rest on, and the answers Josh gave on 22 Oct 2026. Part of [[Friendstead]].

## Working assumptions (base case)

| Assumption | Base case | Why this value |
|---|---|---|
| Households | 6, in three price tiers | Two 2-acre, two 3-acre and two 5-acre homesites, 20 acres of lots plus a 10-acre common tract; same 2,000 sq ft home at every tier, land share differs |
| Tract | 30 acres, unincorporated, outside any ETJ; east side and Hill Country both modeled | East at $28,000 an acre is the base; Hill Country at $60,000 in the sensitivity table **[S2]** |
| Lot size | 2 to 5 acres as the county allows | See [[Jurisdiction and Feasibility]] |
| Home | 2,000 sq ft, standard finish; stick-built is the base | See [[Build Systems Compared]] |
| Occupancy | All primary residences | Homestead and the appraisal cap on every lot after the deed |
| Shared barndominium | 3,500 sq ft on two floors (56 x 40 ft slab, 20 ft eave), dried-in shell with rough interiors, permitted residential | Phase 1 housing, later workshop and community building |
| Road | 2,000 linear feet, paved if the county requires it | A 30-acre tract is roughly 1,100 by 1,200 feet |
| Member labor | Framing, siding, roofing, insulation, drywall, paint, flooring, trim, landscaping, cleanup; no trade licenses | Licensed trades hired, except an owner on their own house |
| Financing | Land bought with 35% down on a land loan; homes built with cash plus construction loans after the deed | Fannie and Freddie do not buy vacant-land loans; land loans need 20 to 50% down **[S42]** |

## Answers received 22 Oct 2026

1. Household count stays flexible; lots of 2 to 5 acres by jurisdiction, at several price points for different incomes.
2. Both east side and Hill Country stay under study.
3. All primary residences.
4. Compare three build systems at 2,000 sq ft with a material and labor split, energy at one and ten years, and a member-versus-hired labor matrix.
5. No trade licenses in the group, possibly later.
6. 2,000 sq ft, standard finish.
7. Also wanted: code and tax savings (unfinished-house valuation, agricultural exemptions).
