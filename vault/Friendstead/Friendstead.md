# Friendstead

A friend-group self-build community on 20 to 40 acres near Austin, Texas. Research and legal planning, no land under contract. Working doc lives in Claude Docs: [Friendstead Structure and Market Analysis](https://claude.ai/artifact/GPnPpzM6q64GkTv3kwCPKM). Repo: `/data/Dev/HousingCoop`.

> [!abstract] The through-line
> **Development LLC → plat → fee-simple lots plus a Chapter 209 POA for the common tract.** Not a Chapter 251 cooperative, not tenants in common.
>
> East-side base case: the co-op self-build lands at **$650,000 to $695,000 per household** against **$770,000** to build the same 2,000 sq ft home alone on a bought lot **[M]**. The saving is sweat equity; without it the co-op costs more than going alone. A barndominium takes another $70,000 to $110,000 off **[M]**.
>
> Labor is counted in **hours only, never dollars**, so it is not taxable barter; a shortfall raises that household's cash share of shared costs. Finished homes sit behind the Texas rural homestead; shared assets sit in the LLC, then the POA.

## Designs

Blender and IFC models of the buildings are in the repo at `/data/Dev/HousingCoop/designs/` (Bonsai in Blender 4.5 LTS). First up: the shared barndominium, now v5: 70 ft east-west by 40 ft, 10 ft walls, 4:12 gable, full-length covered porch on the south. The shop is a 20 ft full-depth bay at the west gable with a 12 x 8 roll-up on the gable and a man door onto the porch. The 50 x 40 house is a split plan with a shared wet core: bedrooms, bath 1 and laundry beside the shop, a 25 ft great room vaulted to the ridge in the centre, master suite and utility at the east end, every fixture on one 48 ft sewer trench, 9 ft flat ceilings over the wings, and a 24 ft timber-frame entry gable over the porch door and great-room windows. Cash estimate about $256,000 on the build-comparison rates **[M]**. Two stories were judged not cheaper for a metal building (see the decision log). Exterior is PVDF bright white roof and bone white walls for solar reflectance (SRI 80+). Generated from a parametric script. Renders land in `exports/` and are linked from here. Playable first-person walkthrough of v5: https://claude.ai/artifact/HsUtQdhz5YpM2NvhuqUwLT (local copy in `exports/barndominium-v5-walkthrough.html`).

## Status
