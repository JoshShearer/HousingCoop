# Friendstead

A friend-group self-build community on 20 to 40 acres near Austin, Texas. Research and legal planning, no land under contract. Working doc lives in Claude Docs: [Friendstead Structure and Market Analysis](https://claude.ai/artifact/GPnPpzM6q64GkTv3kwCPKM). Repo: `/data/Dev/HousingCoop`.

> [!abstract] The through-line
> **Development LLC → plat → fee-simple lots plus a Chapter 209 POA for the common tract.** Not a Chapter 251 cooperative, not tenants in common.
>
> East-side base case: the co-op self-build lands at **$650,000 to $695,000 per household** against **$770,000** to build the same 2,000 sq ft home alone on a bought lot **[M]**. The saving is sweat equity; without it the co-op costs more than going alone. A barndominium takes another $70,000 to $110,000 off **[M]**.
>
> Labor is counted in **hours only, never dollars**, so it is not taxable barter; a shortfall raises that household's cash share of shared costs. Finished homes sit behind the Texas rural homestead; shared assets sit in the LLC, then the POA.

## Designs

Blender and IFC models of the buildings are in the repo at `/data/Dev/HousingCoop/designs/` (Bonsai in Blender 4.5 LTS). First up: the shared barndominium, now v6 (24 Oct 2026), reset to the cheapest functional building: 56 ft east-west by 40 ft, 20 ft eave, 4:12 gable, no porch. The west 24 ft bay is the shop, open to the roof, with two 12 x 12 insulated sectional doors in the west gable. The east 32 x 40 block has a member-built wood second floor on a centre beam line, so the building has about 3,500 sq ft of floor on a 2,240 sq ft slab: a 10 ft core band on one sewer trench with a bunk room, two baths, a laundry closet and a U-stair, one common room and kitchen beside it, five bunk rooms and an open hall above. Closed-cell foam on the whole shell; rough interiors until the final use (guest house plus shop, or whatever the group decides) is settled after the houses. Cash estimate about $234,000 all-in to occupy, $188,000 for the foamed shell with floor and rooms, $131,000 as a bare dry shell **[M]** (itemised with quantities in `designs/barndominium/ESTIMATE-v6.md`, generated by `notes/barndo-v6-estimate.py`). v5 (70 x 40 single storey, vaulted great room, porch, timber entry, about $256,000) is kept as the finished-house alternative; its walkthrough: https://claude.ai/artifact/HsUtQdhz5YpM2NvhuqUwLT. Exterior stays PVDF bright white roof and bone white walls (SRI 80+). Generated from a parametric script; renders in `exports/`.
