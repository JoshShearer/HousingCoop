# Austin Land and Home Market

Tracts of 15 to 50 acres east of Austin list at roughly $20,000 to $40,000 an acre; the same size in Hays or Williamson lists at $45,000 to $65,000, and Travis higher still **[S2]**. Finished homes are in a buyer's market: the metro median sold for $421,000 in September 2026 at 92.7% of list **[S1]**. Part of [[Friendstead]]; feeds [[Per-Member Cost Model]].

## Land

![[friendstead-land-price-by-county.png]]

Caldwell and Bastrop medians sit at a third to a half of Hays, Williamson and Travis, and even their highest listings stay under the western counties' medians. The dashed line is what large ranch tracts trade for across the region: the Austin–Waco–Hill Country regional average was **$8,040 an acre in Q2 2026**, up 8.6% on the year, on a typical tract of 199 acres **[S3]**. A 30-acre tract near Austin therefore carries a three- to ten-fold small-tract premium, and the premium grows as the tract shrinks: a 5-acre lot in eastern Williamson listed at $51,000 an acre against $41,000 to $45,000 for 10 to 19 acres nearby **[S2]**. Buying one 30-acre tract and dividing it is itself a saving over six separate 5-acre lots.
