Pasted into the session by Josh on 2026-10-22; origin unstated (reads as AI-assisted legal planning memo). Full text was pasted with encoding damage; this file keeps the substance. Analyzed in the Claude Doc "Friendstead Structure and Market Analysis".

## Core recommendation
Development LLC buys the tract -> plat/subdivide -> individually deeded homesites + separately owned common tract -> recorded common-facility/road/utility agreement + mutual labor agreement. Compare against a Business Organizations Code ch. 251 cooperative before finalizing.

## Points raised
1. LLC avoids partition risk (Prop. Code 23.001 lets any co-owner compel partition of land held as tenants in common).
2. Make the purchase contingent on a feasibility period: platting under Loc. Gov't Code ch. 232; Travis County 5-acre minimum for lots on individual wells; Austin ETJ goes through the city.
3. Shared barndominium: settle the permitted occupancy and the Phase 1/2/3 ownership before building; septic sizing follows the permit.
4. Sweat equity: a Mutual Construction Labor Agreement with labor units per household, credit rates by task, and a cash conversion for shortfalls.
5. Labor is not volunteering: employment, workers' comp and liability exposure; insurance program (GL, WC/occupational accident, builder's risk, equipment, auto, umbrella).
6. Licensed trades (electrical, plumbing) stay licensed; no labor credit for unqualified work.
7. Ten interlocking documents: operating agreement, subscription agreement, development plan, labor agreement, homesite allocation, common facilities declaration, use restrictions, buy-sell, ROFR, construction rules.
8. Voting tiers: majority / two-thirds or 75% / unanimity.
9. Capital-call ladder with named remedies for a member who cannot pay.
10. Parcel equalization: baseline homesite value with credits/debits.
11. Exit provisions: voluntary, death, disability, divorce, bankruptcy, default, expulsion, project failure.
12. Resale: ROFR with 30-60 day match window; recorded covenants (Prop. Code ch. 202 regulates restrictive covenants).
13. Recorded easements for road and utilities.
14. Septic/water geometry: civil engineer before choosing the property.
15. Western Travis environmental constraints (BCCP habitat, FEMA, USACE 404, LCRA).
16. Mechanic's lien and homestead formalities (Prop. Code 53.254).
17. Contingency matrix of 40-60 events.
