How it is computed

Method and assumptions
  • Land loan = tract price less the down payment. Interest is carried at the stated rate until the lots are released; each lot's release payoff is the loan divided by the final headcount.
  • Capital calls = (engineering, legal, road, electric, barndominium, septic) plus contingency, divided by households. Pre-formation money is at risk if feasibility fails.
  • Project value at cost = the household's land at cost (its lot plus its share of the common tract) + home + utilities + soft costs. Appraised value applies the uplift. The lender advances at most (100% less equity) of appraised value; whatever the household's land equity does not cover is extra cash down.
  • Payment = principal and interest on the loan over the term plus taxes and insurance on value. Income needed = 12 × (payment + other debt, plus the household's share of land-loan interest if the lender counts the guarantee) ÷ the debt-to-income cap.
  • Founders split pre-formation, closing, Phase A calls (engineering and legal) and the land carry among themselves until the group is full; Phase B (road, electric, barndominium) only if the box is ticked. Refund = founder outlay less the same items at the full-strength share, assuming later seats are sold at cost plus carrying costs.
  • Guarantee exposure is shown pro rata and joint and several. Most land lenders require joint and several guarantees from every member of an LLC borrower.
  • Defaults: Friendstead Per-Member Cost Model (24 Oct 2026); land-loan terms from Herring Bank's Texas land-loan guide (35 to 50% down on raw land, about 7.5 to 7.75%); construction terms are typical 2026 ranges from lender guides (20 to 30% equity, 680 score, 43 to 45% debt-to-income, six months reserves). Confirm every lending figure with a lender in writing.
Texas lenders generally refuse the homeowner as contractor on homestead property, so each household also needs a builder of record or an owner-builder lender. Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac do not buy land loans, and a take-out mortgage on a private road needs a recorded road maintenance agreement. Those are gates the calculator cannot score.
